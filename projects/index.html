Closed-loop hunting paradigm (2024)

How do you study naturalistic hunting behavior in a laboratory setting? At the Meister Lab (Caltech), I contributed to a closed-loop system that let a NeuroPixels-implanted mouse pursue a “robotic prey,” a chocolate chip that evaded the mouse with probabilistic strategies borrowed from escapology research (Domenici et al., 2008). This system allowed us to ask questions of how mouse perform task-switching and execute diverse strategies in response to dynamic external and internal states.

The blind men and the molecule (2023)

A graph neural network (GNN) learns the structure of a molecule the way the blind men learned about the elephant: because of its limited receptive field, each atom only feels its neighbors. Attaching expert-crafted descriptors to the learned representation gives the GNN global features that are hard to infer from atomic features alone. At the Meiler Lab (Vanderbilt University), I tested whether combining the two could identify active drug candidates more accurately, with the aim of speeding up computer-aided drug discovery.
