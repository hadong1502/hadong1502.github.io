Research Experience

  • 2025 - 2026
    Gregory S. Call Research Fellow
    Amherst College, Department of Neuroscience, Amherst, MA
    Advisor: Dr. Josef G. Trapani
    • Developed a circuit model of the temporal dynamics of C-bends during larval zebrafish startle responses, integrating field potential, calcium imaging, and behavioral data for an honors thesis.
    • Supported electrophysiology and imaging experiments investigating the effects of low-dose cisplatin on hair-cell function and mitochondrial morphology.
  • 2025
    Research Intern
    Harvard University, Center for Brain Science, Cambridge, MA
    Advisors: Dr. Venkatesh Murthy, Dr. Jacob Zavatone-Veth
    • Developed an inverse inference algorithm to extract latent finite-state controller policies and memories from behavioral data.
    • Designed a behavioral paradigm and collected data to investigate how conflicting auditory and olfactory signals influence trail-following behavior.
  • 2023 - 2025
    Undergraduate Researcher
    Vanderbilt University, Department of Chemistry, Nashville, TN
    Advisors: Dr. Jens Meiler, Dr. Yunchao Liu
    • Developed WelQrate, an open-source benchmark comprising nine high-throughput screening datasets, a standardized chemical-curation pipeline, and benchmarking frameworks for probabilistic, sequence-based, and graph-based machine-learning models.
    • Developed and evaluated a graph-learning augmentation method integrating expert-crafted molecular descriptors into graph representations, improving predictive performance across multiple graph neural-network architectures.
  • 2024
    R&D Intern
    Alkermes plc, Waltham, MA
    Advisors: Dr. Joerg Bentzien, Dr. Polina Vanyukov, Dr. Shinichiro Wachi
    • Developed an end-to-end machine-learning pipeline using ChEMBL data and random-forest models to predict multiple bioactivity endpoints for serotonin (5-HT) and dopamine receptors.
  • 2024
    Summer Undergraduate Research Fellow
    California Institute of Technology, Pasadena, CA
    Advisor: Dr. Markus Meister
    • Helped design and engineer a closed-loop behavioral system in which artificial prey evaded hunting mice using naturalistic escape strategies.
    • Collected Neuropixels and keypoint-tracking data and applied hidden Markov models to characterize internal states underlying hunting behavior and their relationship to superior colliculus activity.
